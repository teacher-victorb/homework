Calling in sick

Why you should do it even if you work from home.

1. The increase in people working from home due to coronavirus looks likely to be here to stay. This brings many benefits to both employees and employers but also means more people working while ill. This is not good for people’s health and will require companies to encourage their employees to take time off when necessary.

2. Working from home allows employees to balance home responsibilities with work demands. The benefits to organizations include people working harder and staff who are more flexible with their time.

3. People who work from home often take fewer sick days than office-based staff. Many actually appreciate the ability to work from home while ill, as it allows them to keep working while avoiding the difficulty of commuting to the office or working a full day. It also prevents workers spreading illnesses to their colleagues.

4. So working when ill, or "sickness presenteeism," is not always bad. The reasons for it include: the employee’s need for money, if there is paid sick leave, how much work there is to do, tight deadlines and how worried the employee is about keeping their job.

5. When it is voluntary, sickness presenteeism can have other positive benefits. If employees have a long-term illness and want to work despite feeling sick, flexible working or homeworking can help them stay in work. This also allows companies to keep staff.

6. But if people feel they must go to work despite being ill, research suggests it can have negative consequences for the employee and the company.

7. A number of studies found that working while sick can mean poor health in the future. It also increased the risk of workers having to take more time off due to sickness 18 months later.

8. Sickness presenteeism also has consequences for your mental health. Research shows that if someone had worked while ill in the previous three months, people felt down or irritable and found it hard to make decisions.

9. Meanwhile, other research has found that working while sick increased the risk of depression two years later, even though the workers were not depressed at first.

10. Companies want to keep employees’ absences as low as possible. Presenteeism is often viewed positively if the alternative is sick leave, as employees will get some work done and their role will not need to be covered by co-workers.

11. But taking sick leave is not a decision that people make easily. In one study 60% of the participants had attended work while unwell in the previous 12 months. People reported working when they were ill, and sometimes, managers had to send people home.

12. In many cases, employees can still work with minor illnesses such as colds, and it will not harm their health in the long term. But workers and employers need to be aware of the possible health risk of working through health conditions that require rest and time to recover.

13. Just because we can work from home when we are ill, doesn’t always mean that we should - and that includes logging on to our laptop or checking your email from the sick bed.
